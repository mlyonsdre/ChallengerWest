About Our Association

Get to know the people and objectives behind our community. This section introduces our mission, the volunteer board members who serve our neighborhood, and provides access to our application and materials submitted to the City of Sunnyvale on the communities behalf.

Our Mission

The Challenger West Neighborhood Association is dedicated to enhancing the quality of life and fostering a strong sense of community for all of our homeowners and residents. We strive to advocate for our shared interests, have fun, support each other, and organize community events.
